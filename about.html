About the Shop 💜

Hi! I'm Mazel, the creator of this little Love and Deepspace shop. I created this shop for fellow fans who enjoy collecting cute, stylish, and character-inspired items.

I wanted to create a fun place where fans can find merchandise inspired by their favorite characters, from soft plushies and figures to fashionable items and accessories.
